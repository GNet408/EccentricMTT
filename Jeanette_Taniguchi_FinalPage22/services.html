Studio Policies

  • Deposits are required for custom or large pieces.
  • Deposits are non‑refundable if not cancelled or rescheduled within one week.
  • Rescheduling must occur at least one week prior or deposit is forfeited.
  • Clients must be 18+ with valid ID.
  • No minors — no exceptions.
  • No refunds on deposits.
  • Harassment of any kind will not be tolerated.

Before Your Appointment

  • Eat beforehand and stay hydrated.
  • Avoid alcohol before your session.
  • Wear comfortable clothing that allows access to the tattoo area.
  • Bring reference images if you have them.
  • Bring a positive attitude — this is your therapy session.
